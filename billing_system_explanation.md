### 1. Dynamic Pricing with Snapshots

The core of the system is the relationship between `pricing_plans` and `pricing_snapshots`.

*   **`pricing_plans`**: This table defines the existence of a plan (e.g., "Basic", "Pro").
*   **`pricing_snapshots`**: This is the crucial table. It stores the actual pricing details for a plan at a specific point in time. It contains:
    *   `pricing_plan_id` to link to a specific plan.
    *   Pricing details like `price_per_message_cents`, `price_per_gb_cents`, etc.
    *   A `pricing_mode` which can be `'postpaid'` or `'prepaid'`.
    *   `valid_from` and `valid_until` timestamps.

This "snapshot" system is designed to handle price changes. When a plan's price is updated, a new entry is created in `pricing_snapshots` with a new `valid_from` date, and the old entry is given a `valid_until` date. This ensures that existing customers are billed at the rate they signed up for, and new customers get the new rate.

### 2. Subscription and Billing Cycle

*   A developer (`user`) creates an `application` and chooses which `pricing_plans` to offer via the `application_pricing_plans` table.
*   A `client` subscribes to a plan, creating a record in `client_subscriptions`.
*   The client's billing cycle is defined in the `billing_periods` table, which sets a `start_date` and `end_date` for each billing period.

### 3. Usage, Invoicing, and Payment Models

The system supports both prepaid and postpaid models:

**A) Postpaid Model (`pricing_mode: 'postpaid'`):**

1.  **Usage Tracking**: As the `client` uses the service, their consumption (messages, data, etc.) is recorded in `client_usage_metrics` and aggregated into `client_billing_usage` for each billing period.
2.  **Invoicing**: At the end of a `billing_period`, a `client_invoice` is generated. The amount is calculated by combining the usage from `client_billing_usage` with the pricing details from the relevant `pricing_snapshot` (the one that was active during the billing period).

**B) Prepaid Model (`pricing_mode: 'prepaid'`):**

1.  **Credits**: When a client subscribes to a prepaid plan, they purchase credits. This is logged as a `credit` transaction in the `credit_transactions` table, and their balance is updated in `client_usage_credits`.
2.  **Consumption**: As they use the service, their usage is still tracked in `client_usage_metrics`. However, instead of generating an invoice at the end of the period, `debit` transactions are created in `credit_transactions`, and their credit balance in `client_usage_credits` is reduced.

### 4. Developer Revenue

The `developer_revenue_shares` table is a key part of the business model. It indicates that developers earn a share of the money generated by their applications. For each `billing_period`, the system calculates the `total_revenue_cents` for an application, applies the developer's `share_percentage`, and tracks the `amount_paid_cents` to the developer.

### Summary of the Flow

This is a robust and flexible billing system that can be summarized as follows:

**Pricing is not static**. It's versioned through **`pricing_snapshots`**. This allows for prices to change without affecting existing subscribers. The system supports both **postpaid** (bill-at-end-of-cycle) and **prepaid** (pay-with-credits) models. All client usage is meticulously tracked and aggregated. At the end of a billing cycle, the system either generates an invoice for postpaid clients or decrements credits for prepaid clients. Finally, the platform shares the revenue with the application developers.
